# The mouse and the cursor

The **cursor** (or **pointer**) is the small arrow on the screen. It moves when you move the mouse.

## Holding the mouse

Rest your hand gently on the mouse. Your first finger rests on the **left button**, your middle finger on the **right button**, and the wheel sits between them.

## Mouse actions

- **Point** — move the pointer over something without pressing a button.
- **Click** — press and release the **left** button once. It selects an item or presses a button.
- **Double-click** — click the left button twice, very fast. It opens a file or folder.
- **Right-click** — press the **right** button. A menu of choices appears.
- **Drag and drop** — press and hold the left button on an item, move it, then let go.
- **Scroll** — roll the wheel to move up and down a page.

## The cursor changes shape

- An **arrow** means you can point and click.
- A **hand** means you are over a link.
- An **I-beam** (a thin line shaped like a capital I) means you are over text, where you can click and type.
- A **spinning circle** means the computer is busy. Wait.

## Selecting text

- **Double-click** a word to select the word.
- **Triple-click** to select the whole line or paragraph.
- **Click and drag** to select exactly what you want.

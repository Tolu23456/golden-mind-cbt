# How to touch type

**Touch typing** means typing without looking at the keys. It makes you faster and you make fewer mistakes.

## The home row

Rest your fingers on these keys:

- Left hand: [kbd:A] [kbd:S] [kbd:D] [kbd:F]
- Right hand: [kbd:J] [kbd:K] [kbd:L] [kbd:;]
- Both thumbs rest on the [kbd:Space] bar.

Feel the small bumps on [kbd:F] and [kbd:J]. They help your fingers find home without looking.

## Rules for good typing

1. Sit up straight, with your feet flat on the floor.
2. Keep your wrists level and relaxed, not bent.
3. Look at the screen, not at your hands.
4. Use the finger that is closest to the key, then return to the home row.
5. Type slowly and correctly first. Speed comes later.

## Speed and accuracy

- **WPM** means words per minute. One word counts as five letters.
- **Accuracy** is the percentage of keys you hit correctly.

> Aim for 95% accuracy before you try to go faster.

Practise a little every day. Ten minutes a day is better than one hour once a week.

# Getting to know the keyboard

## The main areas

- **Letter keys** — A to Z.
- **Number row** — 0 to 9, above the letters.
- **Number pad** — on the right of a full keyboard, for fast number typing.
- **Function keys** — F1 to F12 along the top.
- **Arrow keys** — move the cursor up, down, left and right.

## Special keys

- [kbd:Enter] starts a new line or confirms a choice.
- [kbd:Space] puts a gap between words.
- [kbd:Backspace] deletes the letter **before** the cursor.
- [kbd:Delete] deletes the letter **after** the cursor.
- [kbd:Shift] makes capital letters and the symbols on top of the number keys.
- [kbd:Caps Lock] keeps all your letters capital until you press it again.
- [kbd:Tab] jumps forward, for example to the next box in a form.
- [kbd:Esc] cancels or closes something.

## Shortcuts

A **shortcut** uses two or more keys together. Hold [kbd:Ctrl] and press a letter.

- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:C] copies
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:V] pastes
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:X] cuts
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:Z] undoes your last action
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:S] saves your work
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:A] selects everything

# What is a computer?

A **computer** is a machine that accepts information, stores it, works on it and shows you the result. You give it **input**, it **processes** the input, and it gives you **output**.

## Input, process, output

1. **Input** — what you put in, like typing a word or clicking a button.
2. **Process** — the computer works on it using its brain, the CPU.
3. **Output** — what you get back, like words on the screen or a printed page.

> Example: you press the letter [kbd:A] (input). The computer understands it (process). The letter A appears on the screen (output).

## What can computers do?

- Write letters and school projects
- Do calculations very fast
- Store thousands of photos, songs and documents
- Connect us to the internet to learn, play and talk to people
- Run exams, like a **computer-based test (CBT)**

## Types of computer

- **Desktop** — stays on a table. It has a separate screen, keyboard and mouse.
- **Laptop** — folds up and has a battery, so you can carry it.
- **Tablet** — a flat touch screen.
- **Smartphone** — a small computer that fits in your pocket.

## Remember

A computer only does what people tell it to do. It is fast, but it does not think for itself.

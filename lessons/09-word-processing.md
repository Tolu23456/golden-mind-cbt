# Word processing

A **word processor** is a program for writing documents: letters, essays, reports, forms and CVs. Microsoft Word is the most famous, but Google Docs, LibreOffice Writer and WPS Writer all work in the same basic way.

## Opening and saving

1. Open the program and choose **Blank document**.
2. Type your work.
3. Press [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:S] to save. Give the file a clear name such as `Ada-CV-2026`.
4. Choose a folder you will remember, then press Save.

> Save early, save often. A document that is not saved does not exist.

## The parts of the window

- **Title bar** — the file name and the program name.
- **Ribbon or toolbar** — buttons for bold, colour, lists and more.
- **Ruler** — shows margins and indents.
- **Writing area** — the white page where you type.
- **Status bar** — page number, word count and zoom.

## Making text look right

Select the words first, then apply the change.

- **Bold** makes a word stand out. Use it for headings or important terms.
- *Italic* is for titles of books, foreign words or gentle emphasis.
- Underline is best saved for links.
- **Font size** — 11 or 12 for body text, 24 and above for a title.
- **Alignment** — left for normal writing, centre for titles, right for dates.
- **Line spacing** — 1.15 or 1.5 is easier to read than single spacing.

## Lists and headings

Use the **bulleted list** button for items that do not need an order. Use the **numbered list** button for steps. Do not type dashes and numbers yourself; the list tools keep the spacing tidy.

For long documents, use the **Heading 1**, **Heading 2** and **Heading 3** styles. They make the document easy to scan and let you build a table of contents automatically.

## Checking your work

- The red wavy line marks a spelling mistake. Right-click the word for suggestions.
- The blue or green line marks a grammar issue.
- Press [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:F] to find a word and [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:H] to replace it.
- Read the document once more before printing or sending it.

> Spell check is a helper, not a teacher. It will not notice that you wrote "form" when you meant "from".

## Printing and sharing

Press [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:P] to print. In the print window, check the printer, the number of copies and whether you need one side or both sides.

To share, save as **PDF** so the layout stays the same on every computer, then attach the PDF to an email or upload it to cloud storage.

## Useful shortcuts

- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:S] — save
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:Z] — undo
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:Y] — redo
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:B] — bold
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:C] — copy
- [kbd:Ctrl] + [kbd:V] — paste

## Try it

Write a one-page letter to a school asking for your results. Give it a heading, a date, two short paragraphs and your name at the end. Save it as a PDF.

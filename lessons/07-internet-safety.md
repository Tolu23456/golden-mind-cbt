# Staying safe online

The internet is wonderful for learning, but you must be careful, just like when you cross a road.

## Strong passwords

A **password** keeps your account private.

- Use at least 8 characters, mixing capital letters, small letters, numbers and symbols.
- Never use your name, your birthday or `12345678`.
- **Never share** your password with friends.
- Use a different password for each account.

## Private information

Do not post or tell strangers online:

- your home address
- your phone number
- your school's name and timetable
- your passwords

## Scams and strangers

- A **scam** is a trick to take your money or information. Be careful of messages that say "You have won a prize!"
- Never click a link or open an attachment from someone you do not know.
- If a stranger messages you, do not reply. Tell a trusted adult.

## Be kind

- Think before you post. Once it is online, it can stay there.
- **Cyberbullying** is being unkind to someone online. Do not do it, and tell an adult if you see it.

> If something online makes you feel scared or uncomfortable, close it and tell a trusted adult straight away.

# Taking a CBT exam

A **CBT** (computer-based test) is an exam you take on a computer instead of on paper. Many national exams in Nigeria now use CBT, so this skill matters.

## Before the exam

- Practise using the mouse and keyboard until they feel natural.
- Know how to **scroll** and how to **select** an answer.
- Arrive early and keep your examination number safe.

## During the exam

1. **Read** the instructions on the first screen carefully.
2. Click the **circle** next to your chosen answer. You can usually change it before you submit.
3. Use **Next** and **Previous** to move between questions, or click a question number in the **navigator**.
4. **Flag** a hard question and come back to it later.
5. Watch the **timer**, but do not panic.
6. Do not refresh the page and do not press random keys.

## Managing time

- Answer easy questions first.
- Do not spend more than a minute on one question at first.
- Leave a few minutes at the end to check flagged questions.

## Submitting

When you click **Submit**, the computer marks your test. You cannot change your answers afterwards, so check first.

> Stay calm. Take a slow breath. You have practised this.

Try the **Practice Test** page in **Exam** mode to rehearse.

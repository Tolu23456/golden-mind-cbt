# Parts of a computer

## Hardware

**Hardware** is every part of the computer that you can touch.

- **Monitor** — the screen. It shows you pictures and words (output).
- **System unit** — the box that holds the important parts inside, including the **CPU**.
- **Keyboard** — used to type letters, numbers and commands (input).
- **Mouse** — moves the pointer on the screen (input).
- **Printer** — puts words and pictures on paper (output).
- **Speakers** — play sound (output).

## The CPU

The **CPU** (Central Processing Unit) is the brain of the computer. It does the thinking and the calculating.

## Memory and storage

- **RAM** is short-term memory. It holds what you are working on right now, and it is cleared when the computer is switched off.
- **Storage** (hard drive, SSD, flash drive) keeps your files even when the power is off.

## Software

**Software** is the set of instructions that tells the hardware what to do. You cannot touch it.

- **Operating system** — runs the whole computer. Windows is an example.
- **Applications** — programs for a job, like Word for writing or a browser for the internet.

> Easy way to remember: hardware is the body, software is the instructions that make the body work.

## Starting and stopping

Always **shut down** properly from the Start menu. Do not just pull the plug, because you can lose your work.

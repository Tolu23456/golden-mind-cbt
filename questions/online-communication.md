# Online Communication

## What does typing in ALL CAPITALS usually feel like?

- [ ] Whispering
- [x] Shouting
- [ ] Singing
- [ ] A formal greeting

> Capitals remove nuance and can sound aggressive. Reserve them for short emphasis.

## When should you use "Reply all"?

- [ ] Every time you reply
- [x] Only when everyone in the thread needs your answer
- [ ] When you want to annoy people
- [ ] When the message is private

> Reply to the sender unless the whole group needs the information.

## In a video call, what should you do when you are not speaking?

- [ ] Shout into the microphone
- [x] Mute yourself
- [ ] Leave the camera on yourself eating
- [ ] Play music

> Muting reduces background noise for everyone.

## What is good practice before sharing a private message as a screenshot?

- [ ] Share it immediately
- [x] Ask the person and hide anything private
- [ ] Add it to a group chat
- [ ] Post it publicly

> Forwarding without consent damages trust and can be harmful.

## Which channel suits a difficult or emotional conversation?

- [ ] A public comment
- [ ] A disappearing group chat
- [x] A call or a face-to-face meeting
- [ ] An all-caps message

> Voice and presence reduce misunderstanding.

## What is the best response to online abuse?

- [ ] Reply with worse abuse
- [ ] Share the abuser's details
- [x] Block, report and tell a trusted adult, saving evidence
- [ ] Ignore it forever and tell nobody

> Stay safe, keep evidence and involve someone who can help.

## Which email subject line is most useful?

- [ ] "Hi"
- [ ] "Question"
- [x] "Request for my Computer test score"
- [ ] Leave it blank

> A clear subject tells the reader what to expect and helps later searching.

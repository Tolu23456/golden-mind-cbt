# Staying Safe Online

## Which is the strongest password?
- [ ] 12345678
- [ ] password
- [ ] your name
- [x] A mix of letters, numbers and symbols that is long
> Longer and mixed passwords are harder to guess.

## Who should you share your password with?
- [ ] Your best friend
- [x] Nobody, except maybe a parent or guardian
- [ ] Anyone who asks
- [ ] Everyone in class
> Keep your password private.

## A stranger online asks for your home address. What should you do?
- [ ] Send it
- [x] Do not reply, and tell a trusted adult
- [ ] Send your school address instead
- [ ] Ask for their address first
> Never give personal details to strangers.

## You get a message saying "You won a million naira! Click here!" What is it most likely to be?
- [ ] A real prize
- [x] A scam
- [ ] A school notice
- [ ] A game
> Prizes you did not enter for are usually scams.

## What should you do before opening an attachment?
- [x] Make sure it is from someone you know and trust
- [ ] Open it quickly
- [ ] Forward it to everyone
- [ ] Delete your files
> Unknown attachments can carry viruses.

## What is cyberbullying?
- [ ] Reading online
- [x] Being unkind or threatening to someone online
- [ ] Playing a game
- [ ] Typing quickly
> It is never okay. Tell an adult.

## What should you do if you see something online that scares you?
- [ ] Share it
- [ ] Keep it secret
- [x] Close it and tell a trusted adult
- [ ] Reply angrily
> Telling an adult is the brave choice.

## Which of these is private information?
- [ ] Your favourite colour
- [x] Your home address and phone number
- [ ] Your favourite food
- [ ] The weather today
> Keep addresses and numbers private.

## What is a virus?
- [x] A harmful program that can damage a computer
- [ ] A kind of keyboard
- [ ] A useful tool
- [ ] A type of screen
> Antivirus programs help protect you.

## Before you post a photo online you should:
- [ ] Post it quickly
- [x] Think about who could see it, and ask a trusted adult
- [ ] Add your address
- [ ] Tag everyone you know
> Things posted online can stay there for a long time.

## Why should you log out on a shared computer?
- [ ] To make it go faster
- [x] So other people cannot use your account
- [ ] To save electricity only
- [ ] It is not important
> Always log out in a school or café.

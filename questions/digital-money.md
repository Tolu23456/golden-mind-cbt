# Digital Money

## What should you never share with anyone?

- [ ] Your favourite colour
- [x] Your PIN and one-time codes
- [ ] The name of your school
- [ ] Your typing speed

> No genuine bank or support agent needs your PIN or OTP.

## Before confirming a transfer, what should you check?

- [ ] Only the amount
- [ ] The colour of the app
- [x] The account number, bank, name and amount
- [ ] The time of day

> Check every detail on the confirmation screen. Transfers are hard to reverse.

## Why is a bank transfer difficult to undo?

- [ ] Because banks close at noon
- [x] Because the money leaves your account and the receiver controls it
- [ ] Because phones cannot reverse payments
- [ ] Because receipts are illegal

> Treat every Send button as final.

## A stranger says they sent you money by mistake and asks for a refund. What is the safest response?

- [ ] Send the money back quickly
- [ ] Share your PIN so they can check
- [x] Check your account in the official app and report the message
- [ ] Send half the amount

> This is a common scam. Verify inside the official app and never act on the message alone.

## What should you do first if your card is lost?

- [ ] Wait until morning
- [x] Block it through the bank's official app or phone line
- [ ] Post about it online
- [ ] Borrow a friend's card

> Block the card, then check transactions and report anything strange.

## What is a sensible simple budget split?

- [ ] 100% wants
- [x] 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings
- [ ] 90% savings, 10% needs
- [ ] No plan at all

> Adjust the percentages to your real life, but decide before you spend.

## Which message is almost certainly a scam?

- [ ] A receipt from a transfer you made
- [x] "Your account is blocked. Click this link and enter your PIN to reactivate it."
- [ ] A bank notice you requested
- [ ] A statement inside the official app

> Urgency plus a link plus a request for your PIN is a classic combination.

# Study & Career

## What is the best way to take notes?

- [ ] Copy every word exactly
- [x] Write the idea in your own words and add an example
- [ ] Take no notes at all
- [ ] Only highlight the whole page

> Your own words force you to understand. Examples make the idea stick.

## Which revision method is most effective?

- [ ] Reading the notes once
- [ ] Cramming the night before
- [x] Testing yourself over several days
- [ ] Copying the notes again without thinking

> Active recall and spacing beat passive reading.

## What should a CV include?

- [ ] Your password
- [x] Contact details, education, skills and experience
- [ ] Every message you have ever sent
- [ ] A long paragraph about your childhood

> Keep it clear, honest and short. Save it as a PDF before sending.

## Why save a CV as a PDF?

- [ ] It adds animation
- [x] The layout stays the same on another computer
- [ ] It makes the file unreadable
- [ ] It hides your name

> A PDF protects the design from version differences.

## What is a good way to apply for a role?

- [ ] Send the same CV to everyone without reading the advert
- [x] Read the instructions, tailor the CV and check spelling
- [ ] Send it after the deadline
- [ ] Use a funny email address

> Small details decide many applications.

## Why check your public profiles before applying?

- [ ] To count your followers
- [x] Employers and schools may search for you
- [ ] To change your password
- [ ] To make the internet faster

> Your digital footprint is part of your application.

## What should you do when you receive feedback?

- [ ] Argue with all of it
- [ ] Ignore it
- [x] Choose one point to act on and improve
- [ ] Delete your work

> One change at a time is easier to keep.

## Which filename is best for a job application?

- [ ] `cv-final-final2.pdf`
- [ ] `document.pdf`
- [x] `2026-03-14-Ada-Okafor-CV.pdf`
- [ ] `IMG_2043.pdf`

> A date, your name and the document type are easy to find.

# CBT Exam Skills

## What does CBT stand for in this course?
- [ ] Cool Brain Training
- [x] Computer-Based Test
- [ ] Central Bank Transfer
- [ ] Copy Backup Tool
> A CBT is an exam taken on a computer.

## How do you choose an answer in a CBT?
- [ ] Write it with a pen
- [x] Click the option you choose
- [ ] Shout it out
- [ ] Press Shut down
> You usually click a circle or the option itself.

## Can you usually change your answer before you submit?
- [x] Yes
- [ ] No, never
- [ ] Only with a teacher's pen
- [ ] Only after submitting
> Click a different option to change.

## What is the question navigator for?
- [ ] Printing questions
- [x] Jumping straight to any question number
- [ ] Switching off the timer
- [ ] Changing the font
> Click a number to go to that question.

## What should you do with a very difficult question?
- [ ] Panic
- [x] Flag it and come back later
- [ ] Close the exam
- [ ] Click randomly
> Move on and use your time wisely.

## What should you do first when the exam begins?
- [ ] Start clicking
- [x] Read the instructions carefully
- [ ] Refresh the page
- [ ] Switch off the screen
> Instructions explain how this exam works.

## What should you NOT do during a CBT?
- [ ] Watch the timer
- [x] Refresh the page or press random keys
- [ ] Read carefully
- [ ] Stay calm
> Refreshing can cause problems.

## What happens when the timer reaches zero?
- [ ] You get extra time
- [x] The test is submitted automatically
- [ ] Nothing happens
- [ ] The exam starts again
> Manage your time so you finish before it ends.

## Why is practising with a mouse and keyboard important?
- [ ] It is not
- [x] You will be faster and more confident in the real exam
- [ ] The mouse does the exam for you
- [ ] The keyboard gives free marks
> Practice builds confidence.

## What should you do just before you click Submit?
- [x] Check your answers and flagged questions
- [ ] Close your eyes
- [ ] Switch off the computer
- [ ] Leave the room
> You cannot change answers after submitting.

## Which helps you stay calm in an exam?
- [ ] Holding your breath
- [x] Taking slow, steady breaths
- [ ] Rushing
- [ ] Not preparing
> Slow breathing helps you think clearly.

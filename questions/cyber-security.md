# Cyber Security

## What makes a password strong?

- [ ] A short word in capitals
- [ ] Your birthday
- [x] A long, unique passphrase
- [ ] The word "password" followed by 1

> Length and uniqueness matter most. Never reuse an important password.

## What does two-step verification add?

- [ ] A second password sent by email
- [x] A second proof, such as a code from an app
- [ ] A second email address
- [ ] A second browser

> Even a stolen password is not enough without the second step.

## Which is a warning sign of a phishing message?

- [ ] A calm, expected message from a known contact
- [x] Urgent language and a link asking for your password
- [ ] A clear subject line
- [ ] A message you requested

> Urgency, strange links and requests for secrets are classic signs.

## A caller says they are from your bank and asks for the OTP you just received. What should you do?

- [ ] Read it out to help them
- [ ] Send it by SMS
- [x] Refuse and contact the bank using a number you already know
- [ ] Share your PIN as well

> No real bank needs your one-time code. That request is a scam.

## What is a password manager for?

- [ ] Sharing passwords with friends
- [x] Creating and remembering long, unique passwords
- [ ] Deleting your accounts
- [ ] Speeding up the internet

> You memorise one strong master password; the manager handles the rest.

## Which activity is safest on public Wi-Fi?

- [ ] Checking your bank balance
- [ ] Entering your card details
- [x] Reading a public news site with no login
- [ ] Changing your email password

> Avoid sensitive logins on networks you do not control. Use mobile data instead.

## Where does malware often come from?

- [ ] Official app stores
- [ ] Automatic security updates
- [x] Cracked software and unexpected attachments
- [ ] A trusted antivirus scan

> "Free" downloads and unexpected files are common carriers.

## What is the first thing to do if an account is compromised?

- [ ] Wait a week
- [x] Change the password from a different device and turn on two-step verification
- [ ] Delete the app
- [ ] Tell everyone your old password

> Move quickly, secure the account, then tell the organisation and check for damage.

# Presentations

## What is the best amount of text on a slide?

- [ ] A full paragraph
- [ ] The whole script
- [x] A short title and a few brief points
- [ ] No text at all

> The slide supports your voice. The detail belongs in what you say and in the speaker notes.

## What is the smallest sensible body text size for a projected slide?

- [ ] 10 points
- [ ] 14 points
- [x] 24 points
- [ ] 48 points

> Around 24 points and above keeps the back row comfortable.

## Why does every image need alt text?

- [ ] To make the file smaller
- [x] To describe it for people who cannot see it
- [ ] To change its colour
- [ ] To stop it being copied

> Screen readers announce alt text, and it appears if the image fails to load.

## Which chart best shows a trend over time?

- [ ] Pie chart
- [x] Line chart
- [ ] A single photo
- [ ] A table of every value

> A line chart makes change and direction easy to see.

## What should you do while presenting?

- [ ] Read every slide word for word
- [ ] Turn your back to the audience
- [x] Speak slowly and make eye contact
- [ ] Rush to finish early

> The audience can read. Your job is to explain, not to recite.

## Why export a PDF copy of your slides?

- [ ] It adds animation
- [x] The layout stays the same on another computer
- [ ] It makes the file larger
- [ ] It hides the text

> Fonts and spacing can shift between versions of the presentation program. A PDF is safer.

## What is a good way to prepare for questions?

- [ ] Refuse to answer
- [x] Think about likely questions and prepare short answers
- [ ] Add more text to the slides
- [ ] End early

> It is fine to say "I will find out and get back to you" when you do not know.

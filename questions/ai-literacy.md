# AI & Chatbots

## How does a chatbot produce an answer?

- [ ] It looks the answer up in one official book
- [x] It predicts likely words from patterns in its training data
- [ ] It asks the government
- [ ] It always calculates the truth

> It generates plausible text. Plausible is not the same as correct.

## What is an AI "hallucination"?

- [ ] A screen glitch
- [x] A confident but invented answer
- [ ] A slow internet connection
- [ ] A type of password

> Always check names, dates, numbers and quotations against a reliable source.

## Which information should you never type into a chatbot?

- [ ] A maths question
- [ ] A request to explain photosynthesis
- [x] Your password or bank details
- [ ] A request to improve grammar

> Conversations may be stored or used for training. Keep private data out.

## Which is the strongest prompt?

- [ ] "Explain it."
- [ ] "Help me."
- [x] "Explain photosynthesis in simple English for a first-year student, with one everyday example."
- [ ] "Do my homework."

> Good prompts give context, level, task and format.

## Who is responsible for work you submit?

- [ ] The chatbot
- [ ] The internet provider
- [x] You
- [ ] Nobody

> You must understand and stand behind every idea you submit.

## Why can AI be biased?

- [ ] Because computers are always fair
- [x] Because it learns patterns, including unfair ones, from its training data
- [ ] Because it has feelings
- [ ] Because it is too slow

> Ask who is missing from the data and who is affected by the decision.

## What should you do with an important fact from a chatbot?

- [ ] Trust it because it sounds sure
- [ ] Share it immediately
- [x] Check it against a textbook, teacher or trusted website
- [ ] Ask the same chatbot again and stop

> Confident language is not evidence.

## Which is a good use of AI for schoolwork?

- [ ] Submitting its essay as your own
- [x] Brainstorming ideas and checking your grammar, then writing in your own words
- [ ] Generating fake references
- [ ] Avoiding the lesson

> Follow your school's rules and keep your own voice.
